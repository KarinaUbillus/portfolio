### Project overview

This government-funded initiative aimed to strengthen advanced scientific education and research capabilities in Peru through doctoral training in Natural Resource Economics and Sustainable Development.

The program focused on developing highly qualified professionals with the theoretical knowledge and research skills necessary to address economic, environmental and natural resource management challenges.

### My contribution

Provided administrative and academic program support, contributing to:

- Supporting the administrative coordination of the doctoral program.
- Assisting with academic documentation and institutional records.
- Coordinating communication with faculty members, doctoral students and institutional stakeholders.
- Supporting admissions, registration and other academic administration processes.
- Assisting with the organization and follow-up of academic activities and program requirements.
- Supporting institutional documentation related to academic program development and accreditation.
