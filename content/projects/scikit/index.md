## Project overview

EF-033 — Doctoral Programs at Peruvian Universities (2015-01) supported the development of advanced scientific education and research capabilities at Universidad Nacional Agraria La Molina.

The program focused on doctoral training in Natural Resource Economics and Sustainable Development and the development of professionals capable of addressing economic, environmental, and natural-resource management challenges.

**Executing institution:** Universidad Nacional Agraria La Molina  
**Funding institution:** FONDECYT / CONCYTEC  
**Grant reference:** CONV-000200-2015-FONDECYT-DE  
**Execution period:** January 2016–January 2023

## Funding awarded

- Promotion 1: S/ 2,873,889
- Promotion 2: S/ 2,209,151
- Combined funding: S/ 5,083,040

## Contributions

- Administrative coordination of the doctoral program
- Academic documentation and institutional records
- Communication with faculty, doctoral students, and institutional stakeholders
- Support for admissions and registration processes
- Organization and follow-up of academic activities
- Institutional documentation related to program development and accreditation

This work strengthened collaboration across academic and administrative environments and supported the operational requirements of a publicly funded higher-education initiative.
